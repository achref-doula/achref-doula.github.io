Research

Reasoning and decision making under uncertainty

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Telecooperation Lab, TU Darmstadt, and an affiliated postdoctoral researcher at Tongji University, Shanghai. I completed my PhD in Computer Science at TU Darmstadt in 2025 (summa cum laude), supervised by Prof. Max Mühlhäuser.

My research focuses on reliable reasoning and decision making under uncertainty, particularly conformal prediction, calibration transfer, and reliability under distribution and model shift. My broader work includes neuro-symbolic learning, LLM reasoning, and model evaluation.

I am currently studying how uncertainty can guide human–AI decision making. Emerging directions include possibilistic and imprecise representations of epistemic uncertainty, and game-theoretic models of repeated human–AI interaction.

Research Interests: Uncertainty Quantification & Conformal Prediction · Distribution & Model Shift · Human–AI Decision Making · Game-Theoretic Models of Interaction · LLM Reasoning & Evaluation · Neuro-Symbolic AI
